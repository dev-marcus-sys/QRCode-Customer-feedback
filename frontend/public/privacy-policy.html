1. 引言

華潤隆地有限公司（下稱「本公司」或「華潤隆地」）深知個人資料私隱的重要性，並依據香港法例第 486 章《個人資料（私隱）條例》（下稱「《條例》」）的原則制定本政策。本政策說明本公司透過「客戶意見反饋系統」（下稱「本系統」）收集、使用、保管及處理閣下個人資料的方式；本系統由華潤隆地營運及管理，用以處理所管理屋苑的住戶及公眾意見、投訴與查詢。

China Resources Lung Tak Limited ("the Company" or "CR Lung Tak") values the privacy of personal data and has prepared this Policy in accordance with the principles of the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486, Laws of Hong Kong) (the "Ordinance"). This Policy explains how the Company collects, uses, safeguards and processes your personal data through the "Customer Feedback System" (the "System"). The System is operated and managed by CR Lung Tak to handle feedback, complaints and enquiries from residents and the public of the estates it manages.

2. 我們收集的資料

閣下透過本系統提交意見或查詢時，我們可能收集以下類別的個人資料：

  1. 身分資料：稱謂、姓名；
  2. 聯絡資料：電郵地址、電話號碼；
  3. 住址資料：屋苑／屋邨、座號、樓層、單位（如閣下自願提供）；
  4. 事件資料：發生日期、時間及意見內容；
  5. 其他：閣下選擇接受的後續滿意度調查同意。

When you submit feedback or enquiries through the System, we may collect the following categories of personal data: (a) identity data: title and name; (b) contact data: email address and telephone number; (c) address data: estate, block, floor and unit (if voluntarily provided); (d) incident data: date, time and content of feedback; and (e) preferences: your consent to a follow-up satisfaction survey.

3. 收集資料的目的及用途

我們僅就以下直接相關目的收集及使用閣下的個人資料：

  1. 處理及跟進閣下提交的意見、投訴或查詢；
  2. 就個案進度與閣下聯絡；
  3. 進行服務改善、統計分析及滿意度調查（如閣下同意）；
  4. 履行適用法律及監管要求。

We collect and use your personal data only for the following directly related purposes: (a) handling and following up on your feedback, complaints or enquiries; (b) contacting you regarding case progress; (c) service improvement, statistical analysis and satisfaction surveys (where you have consented); and (d) complying with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

4. 資料的披露

除下列情況外，本公司不會向第三者披露閣下的個人資料：

  1. 獲閣下事先同意；
  2. 向為本公司提供服務的代理人、承辦商或專業顧問（如保安、維修、法律顧問）披露，且該等人士須對資料保密；
  3. 為遵守適用法律、法庭命令或監管機構要求而須披露；
  4. 在集團公司之間為上述目的而共享（如適用）。

We will not disclose your personal data to third parties except: (a) with your prior consent; (b) to agents, contractors or professional advisers (e.g. security, maintenance, legal counsel) who are bound by confidentiality; (c) where required by applicable law, court order or regulator; or (d) within our corporate group for the above purposes (where applicable).

5. 資料保安

本公司依循華潤隆地資訊安全管理體系，採取切實可行的技術及行政措施，防止個人資料遭未經授權的查閱、使用、更改、披露或遺失，包括：傳輸層加密（HTTPS/TLS）、靜態資料加密、基於角色的存取權限控制與最小權限原則、操作紀錄審計、定期漏洞掃描及安全審視，並要求所有獲授權處理資料的員工及承辦商遵守保密義務。

In line with CR Lung Tak's information security management framework, the Company implements practicable technical and administrative measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, use, modification, disclosure or loss. These include encrypted transmission (HTTPS/TLS), encryption at rest, role-based access control with the principle of least privilege, audit logging, periodic vulnerability scanning and security reviews, and binding confidentiality obligations on all authorised staff and contractors handling the data.

6. 資料保留

我們會在達成收集目的所需期間內保留閣下的個人資料，並在完成處理後按內部保留期及法律要求銷毀或匿名化相關資料，一般不超過 [X] 年。

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose of collection, after which it is destroyed or anonymised in accordance with our retention schedule and legal requirements, generally not exceeding [X] years.

7. 閣下的權利

根據《條例》，閣下有權：

  1. 查閱本公司持有閣下的個人資料（及獲取副本）；
  2. 要求更正不準確的個人資料；
  3. 就直接促銷用途撤回同意（如適用）。

如欲行使上述權利，請按第 8 節的聯絡方式提出。本公司會在切實可行的範圍內盡快回覆，並在法律允許下可能收取查閱資料的合理費用。

Under the Ordinance, you have the right to: (a) access your personal data held by us (and obtain a copy); (b) request correction of inaccurate data; and (c) withdraw consent for direct marketing (where applicable). To exercise these rights, contact us per Section 8. We will respond as soon as practicable and may charge a reasonable fee for data access as permitted by law.
